Track 1: 폭풍 (The Storm)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
The wind smells different
Under the salt there is iron
It's coming

[Verse 1]
A morning when the gulls fly low
The wind has changed direction
This knee, forty years on boats
Knows before the sky does, and aches
I went down to the harbour and said it quietly
Tie up the boats, take in the nets
The young men laughed and asked
The sky's this clear, old man
Instead of answering I looked at the sky
By evening they would know

[Hook]
The storm is coming, the storm is coming
The sound of the sea grinding its teeth
The storm is coming, the storm is coming
A ten-year-old seabed turns over

[Verse 2]
At midday the sky turned the colour of ink
And the waves came over the stone wall
Roofs flew, the beams cried out
People ran for the mountain, for the mountain
I sat on the porch and left the door open
Only those with something to run from run
And I watched the sea I'd known forty years
Bare its teeth
I was not afraid
What there was to lose I lost ten years ago

[Hook]
The storm is coming, the storm is coming
The sound of the sea grinding its teeth
The storm is coming, the storm is coming
A ten-year-old seabed turns over

[Verse 3]
In the middle of the storm I spoke to the sea
I did not shout. I said it quietly.
Ten years ago on that night you took my son
That same night a ship went down with him
What ship it was, I did not ask
I asked, and got only the sound of waves
Today I will not ask
Only do one thing
If you have something to take, take me
And if you have something to give back
Give it back now

[Final Hook]
The storm is passing, the storm is passing
The sound of the sea closing its mouth
The storm is passing, the storm is passing
The ten-year-old seabed has turned over

[Outro]
At dawn after the storm
The sea was calm, as if nothing had happened
But I know
The bottom has turned over
What the sea has hidden all this time
It is about to spit out

Track 2: 가라앉은 배 (The Sunken Ship)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
The day after the storm
The sea was too quiet
Quiet like that does not come free

[Verse 1]
I went out at dawn to read the water routes
You have to read a sea a storm has changed
The sand on the shoal had moved
And a familiar rock stood in an unfamiliar place
And then I saw it, at the end of the ebb
A single black bow risen out of the water
The ship I'd heard about in rumours for ten years
It was there. As if it had waited for me.

[Hook]
The sunken ship, the sunken ship
The secret the sea spat out
The sunken ship, the sunken ship
Pretending not to know ends here

[Verse 2]
I rowed closer
My heart beat faster than a janggu
A government brand on the split hull
Seaweed growing on the broken mast
Ten years ago, the night this ship went down
The old fisherman's elder son was in that water
They say the old man prayed inside the storm
If you have something to give back, give it back
The sea has answered
This bow is the answer

[Hook]
The sunken ship, the sunken ship
The secret the sea spat out
The sunken ship, the sunken ship
Pretending not to know ends here

[Verse 3]
All my life I lived pretending not to know
Because a man who knows too much does not live long
So why does the oar not stop
Why does the bow keep turning that way
I know what is down there
Government documents, and human bones
I cannot dive. I am a man of the land.
But I know a woman who dives
A woman whose breath-sound is unusually clear
A woman who knows the sea floor like her own yard

[Bridge]
I will tell the scholar
Because judging is the work of one who knows letters
I will ask the diver
Because going down is the work of one who knows water
I will row the boat
Because that is all I know

[Final Hook]
The sunken ship, the sunken ship
The secret the sea spat out
The sunken ship, the sunken ship
The pretending is over

[Outro]
For ten years I called it the wind
From today I cannot call it the wind
The bow is facing north
I am the one who rowed it there
