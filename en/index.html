TRILOGY ONE

The Fire Trilogy

  1. CH. 1 Blood and Fog A woman dies in the palace with no name and no grave. The king who loved her finds the hand that poisoned her, and from that day signs his decrees in blood instead of ink. READ →
  2. CH. 2 Ink and Embers A scholar exiled to Jeju for writing the truth sends his words back as a clown's song. A woman comes down from the mountain with a torch, and that night beacons rise on every pass. READ →
  3. CH. 3 Ash and Seed The uprising did not last half a day. The scholar to the execution ground, the clown to the road, the woman back to the mountain empty-handed. Forty years later a girl of sixteen finds a sheet of paper beneath the floorboards. READ →

TRILOGY TWO

The Sea Trilogy

  1. CH. 4 Salt and Waves Four people meet on a southern island: another exiled scholar, a smuggler with a black sail, an old fisherman the sea has emptied, and a diver who is master only underwater. That summer she sees a sunken ship on the northern shoal. READ →
  2. CH. 5 Sea and Bones A storm turns the sea floor over. The diver brings up a chest, and inside it are the names of those who bought false testimony. Only now does the scholar understand why he was sent to this island. READ →
  3. CH. 6 Island and Stars Three men raise a white sail and head north. Halfway across, the diver goes back into the water. On a mountain, an old woman remembers faces where the ledger has only names. What happened after that is in no record. READ →

TRILOGY THREE

The Iron Trilogy

  1. CH. 7 Snow and Iron That winter the Jurchen cross the river. The state pardons its criminals and calls on the men who spent their lives running. A blacksmith melts down temple bells, and a woman who can write walks north carrying paper and ink. IN PROGRESS →
  2. CH. 8 Iron and Blood The palace and the rebels stand in the same line. A man who spent his life running stands still for the first time, and a woman who can write records the name of each dying man on the day he dies. Then a sealed decree arrives from the capital. IN PROGRESS →
  3. CH. 9 Spring and Rust They say the war was won, but there is nothing left to plant. An old farmer finds his own son in a frozen field, and that son buries a paper he cannot read inside a salt jar. Five hundred years later, someone digs where the kitchen stood. READ →
