Ok-i

The haenyeo · breath-hold diver

On land she must lower her head. Underwater her skill decides what she can do. She is the first to see the wreck on the northern shoal.

Reveal their later story · spoilers

She brings the chest to the surface. During the voyage to the mainland, she chooses to return to the water, where she has a freedom the land does not give her.
