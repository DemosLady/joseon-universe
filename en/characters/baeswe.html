Bae-swe

The smuggler

A man with no surname who has survived by sailing under a black sail and asking no questions about his cargo. Finding the wreck changes that arrangement.

Reveal their later story · spoilers

He raises a white sail to take the evidence north, then lowers it when war comes. He joins the front line and eventually stands before the king.
