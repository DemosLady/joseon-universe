Jeong-an

The woman at the mountain temple

After a purge destroys her family, she takes Buddhist vows on Songni Mountain. Jeong-an is her religious name; her birth name remains unrevealed.

Reveal their later story · spoilers

She leaves the mountain carrying a torch, then returns after the uprising fails. Later, the travellers from the sea bring her the ledger. She remembers the faces behind its names.
