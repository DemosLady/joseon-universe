Timeline

SIX HUNDRED YEARS · NINE CHAPTERS

The story runs across six centuries. Most of it happens in one lifetime — and then the paper waits.

a thing that happens the paper moves
  1. THE FIRE TRILOGY
  2. year 0

    CH. 1

    Yeon-i dies

    Squeezed out by palace politics, without a single line left about her in the chronicles. The gentle king becomes a tyrant. He starts signing petitions in blood instead of ink.

  3. +30

    CH. 2

    Yun-je is exiled to Jeju

    For the crime of writing the truth. His writing becomes the entertainer Tal-swe's song, and that song becomes the people's roar. The uprising does not last half a day.

  4. +31

    CH. 3

    Yun-je is executed

    One page of his last writing is left under somebody's floorboards. Nobody takes it out for forty years.

  5. +71

    CH. 3

    Bun-i finds that paper

    Sixteen years old, cleaning under the floor. She sits at the teacher's door for three days to be taught her letters.

  6. THE SEA TRILOGY
  7. +75

    CH. 4

    A ship goes down

    On the northern shoal. Inside it, state documents and the bones of twelve men — together with a ledger listing the men who bought false testimony.

  8. +85

    CH. 4

    Su-gyeom arrives in exile and four people meet

    A scholar, the smuggler Bae-swe, the old fisherman Gap-su, and the diver Ok-i. Where the land runs out.

  9. +85

    CH. 5

    Ok-i brings the ledger up

    After the storm turns the seabed over. She leaves her abalone knife behind — today she is not going down to take, but to receive.

  10. +86

    CH. 6

    Three of them sail north

    Ok-i goes back into the water halfway across. The three who remain find Jeong-an on Songni Mountain.

  11. THE IRON TRILOGY
  12. +86

    CH. 7

    The north is breached

    The Jurchen cross the Amnok and three fortresses fall in three days. The state pardons its criminals. Bae-swe lowers his sail with the ledger inside his coat and walks north.

  13. +86

    CH. 7

    Bun-i walks north

    Forty-four years old, carrying nothing but paper and ink — to write down the name of each dying man on the day he dies.

  14. +87

    CH. 8

    Bae-swe stands before the king

    The morning after the fighting. He puts his hand into his coat and takes it out empty. Bun-i stops him.

  15. +87

    CH. 8

    The ledger passes to Bun-i

    Asked how long she will keep it, she answers: until there is someone left to ask.

  16. +87

    CH. 9

    Bun-i hands it to the soldier

    Because he is twenty. Because it looks like he will live a long time. Do not ask what it is, she tells him.

  17. +87

    CH. 9

    The farmer finds his son

    In a frozen field. He thought it was a stone. It was not a stone.

  18. +88

    CH. 9

    The soldier buries it in the salt jar

    In his mother's kitchen. He could not read it, and because he could not read it, he kept it.

  19. +148

    CH. 9

    His granddaughter asks

    Sixty years later. Grandfather, where is that paper? — I don't remember, he says.

  20. +588

    CH. 9

    Someone digs where the kitchen stood

    Five hundred years later. The salt had not spoiled. The seal was still whole.
