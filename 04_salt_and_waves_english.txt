Track 1: 유배길 (Road of Exile)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
Let the criminal hear
For the crime of troubling the realm with a brush
Exile is ordered to an island at the southern edge

[Verse 1]
The gates of Hanyang close behind my back
The dawn fog erases my name
The gat is taken off, the robe is torn
What remains is only ink-stained fingers
Guards' footsteps, the sound of chains
A thousand ri of road laid out beneath my feet
What I wrote was not a lie
It was a kingdom where truth is a crime

[Hook]
Road of exile, road of exile
Southward, southward
Though the brush breaks, the ink does not dry
Road of exile, road of exile
The sea is calling me

[Verse 2]
The rice fields end, the stone walls grow lower
There is salt in the wind now
An old woman at the tavern holds out a bowl of soup
And bows her head without a word
The people know who the real criminal is
Those inside the palace, or me here on the road
They can bind my hands but not my words
In my head there are a thousand sheets of paper

[Hook]
Road of exile, road of exile
Southward, southward
Though the brush breaks, the ink does not dry
Road of exile, road of exile
The sea is calling me

[Verse 3]
I crossed the pass and the world ended
No — where it ended, the sea began
The waves come in and catch my ankles
The horizon reads out my sentence
The boatman rows, the gulls cry
I look back and the mainland is going away
The king has thrown me out
But the sea is taking me in

[Outro]
Though the brush breaks
The ink does not dry

Track 2: 검은 돛 (Black Sail)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
A night with no wind is the best night

[Verse 1]
On a night the moon hides behind cloud
I raise the black sail and row
The harbour lanterns at my back
The bow watching one star only
The navy's patrol boat is snoring
When the world sleeps, I wake
A sack of salt, a bolt of silk
And one letter with no name on it

[Hook]
Black sail, black sail
The sea asks no questions
Black sail, black sail
The waves do not speak
Between island and island
Down a water road only I know
Black sail, black sail
Before the dawn comes

[Verse 2]
What the country blocks, the sea connects
The road the law cut, the water mends
The scales on land tip toward the gentry
But the scales at sea tip toward nerve
I carry banned books too
What is a letter, to stake a life on —
But a scholar who came from the mainland told me
The ink does not dry

[Verse 3]
Tonight a rumour is going round the water road
That a ship went down on the northern shoal
That it was carrying government records
That the truth is sleeping under the sea
I hear it and pretend I didn't
A man who knows too much does not live long
And still my bow keeps turning north —
Let's call that the wind

[Hook]
Black sail, black sail
The sea asks no questions
Black sail, black sail
The waves do not speak
Between island and island
Down a water road only I know
Black sail, black sail
Before the dawn comes

[Outro]
The sea knows everything
It simply doesn't tell

Track 3: 늙은 그물 (Old Net)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
The sea took everything
So why do you still go out to sea
That's what people ask me

[Verse 1]
My eyes open before dawn
Forty years of living this way
Thick-knuckled hands mend the net
You can mend a hole, you cannot mend the years
The storm took the elder one
The fever took the younger one
My wife went first, waiting
So instead of an empty house, I talk to the sea

[Hook]
Old net, old net
The sea gives and the sea takes
Old net, old net
And still, at dawn, I row

[Verse 2]
When I was young I ran up the full-catch flag
And laughed loud enough to shake the harbour
Now I come back with an empty boat
And only the sound of waves fills the hull
Last month a scholar from the mainland
I took him in my boat and carried him to the island
They said he was a criminal, but his eyes were clear
The sea does not sort people

[Hook]
Old net, old net
The sea gives and the sea takes
Old net, old net
And still, at dawn, I row

[Verse 3]
The night the ship went down on the northern shoal
My elder son was in that water too
Government ship or not, I don't know
What happens under the sea, only the sea knows
There's a young woman who dives these days
And when her breath bursts over the water
Without meaning to, I put a hand on my chest
Alive. She's alive again today.

[Outro]
The sea took everything from me
But the sea is all I have left

But underwater I'm the master
A place no nobleman in a gat can reach

I go down carrying one breath
And the sea spreads out my skirt
