Track 1: 첫 줄 (The First Line)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
They told me to stand
I asked where
Here, in this line

[Verse 1]
Thirty years at sea I lived as a man who was not there
Not being where I was supposed to be — that was my trade
When the king's ship came I had already passed
When the light swung round I was already a shadow
And today they tell me to stand
They tell me not to move
Running is the one thing I learned in my life
And they tell me not to use it

[Pre-Chorus]
My feet want to move
Thirty years are soaked into them
So I hold my own feet down and stand
This is my first fight

[Hook]
I stood in the first line
I stood in the first line
There is no going back
Because either side of me is a man
I stood in the first line
I stood in the first line
Not out of courage
Because there was nothing else to do

[Verse 2]
The man beside me does not know who I am
He does not ask where I came from
He is a king's soldier and I am a pardoned thief
And today we are both just a line
His hand shakes. My hand shakes too.
Two shaking hands level the same spear
I will not call this brotherhood
But today I could die beside this man

[Pre-Chorus]
My coat is heavy
A bundle of soaked paper
Names the sea kept for ten years
Nobody knows they are here

[Hook]
I stood in the first line
I stood in the first line
There is no going back
Because either side of me is a man
I stood in the first line
I stood in the first line
Not out of courage
Because there was nothing else to do

[Bridge]
The drum sounded
The line was pushed back
But it did not break
It was not me who held
It was the man beside me who held
And the man beside him
And so I held too
I did not know this before

[Final Hook]
I stood in the first line
I stood in the first line
For the first time in thirty years
I did not run
I stood in the first line
I stood in the first line
Not because I am brave
Because someone was beside me

[Outro]
The sun came up
The man beside me is still standing
I still have not asked his name

Track 2: 두 걸음 (Two Steps)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
The king's command

[Verse 1 — King]
The north has fallen back
You did this
I know that
The pardon will be kept
I promise it in my own name

[Exchange]
Your Majesty
Speak

[Verse 2 — Smuggler]
Yesterday morning a man died
He did not die in the fighting
He died after the fighting ended
He was a pardoned man
Beheaded for stealing salt ten years ago
He died with your paper inside his coat
And your seal was stamped on that paper
So I ask — what is that paper?

[Verse 3 — King]
That pardon covers the battlefield
Outside the battlefield it does not cover
The law is so
It was not I who made that law
But I sit above it
The officer acted according to the law
And so I will not punish that officer

[Break]
I understand

[Verse 4 — Smuggler]
Then I will ask one more thing
When this fighting is over
what are we?
Today we are a line, and tomorrow what?
The man who stands beside you
and the man you pardoned
walked the same ground and bled the same blood
Does that hold tomorrow morning too?
Or tomorrow morning are we
thieves again

[Bridge]
My hand went into my coat
A bundle of soaked paper is there
Names the sea kept for ten years
The names of the men who killed those people
The only person in this world who could take it
is standing in front of me now
Two steps
Two steps is all it would take

[Break]
I took my hand out
It was empty

[Outro]
What is your name?
I have none
Then let it stay that way

Track 3: 이름을 듣소 (I Listen for the Name)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
Bring water
Tear more cloth
And ask that man his name

[Verse 1]
I cannot heal them
Let me say that first
A man whose belly is open I cannot heal
A man losing blood like that, I cannot either
What I do is hold a hand
Wet a cloth and hold it to a mouth
And there is one more thing

[Pre-Chorus]
I put my ear close and listen
I listen for what comes out between the breaths
If I cannot hear it I ask again
Say it once more for me

[Chorus]
I listen for the name
I write the name
Even if I cannot save him
I can make sure somebody knows
The name his mother called him
That is the name I write
Not the number the army gave him
The name they called him at home

[Verse 2]
The paper ran out
I used the last of it yesterday
So I wrote on cloth
And after that on wood
Today I write on the arm of a living man
So that if he dies
someone will look at that arm and read
You write it while he is still alive

[Break]
Park Deok-su
Kim Man-bok
Yi Dol-i

[Pre-Chorus]
At sixteen I learned my letters
I found paper beneath the floorboards
and sat at the door for three days to be taught
I did not know then what it was for

[Chorus]
I listen for the name
I write the name
Even if I cannot save him
I can make sure somebody knows
The name his mother called him
That is the name I write
Not the number the army gave him
The name they called him at home

[Bridge]
A man took my hand and asked
Did you write it?
I said I wrote it
And then he closed his eyes
He did not say thank you
There was no time for that
But I heard it
That "ah"
That one breath

[Final Chorus]
I listen for the name
I write the name
I cannot save them
but I will not let them be forgotten
Fire burns
Salt preserves
Iron remembers
And a person writes

[Outro]
Next
Next man
What is your name?

Track 4: 봉인 (The Seal)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
I hear hooves
One horse
The fighting is over — what is so urgent?

[Verse 1]
Twenty-three men lie here
and I got as far as nineteen
Four I could not ask
Their breath went first
Those four stay as empty lines
And today one horse comes

[Hook]
The seal is still whole
Nobody opens it
Nobody has to open it to know
The seal is still whole

[Verse 2]
He came down off the horse
There is a decree in his hand
Nobody is reading it
These are faces that know without reading
The fighting is over, so a criminal is a criminal again
The pardon covers the battlefield
Outside the battlefield it does not cover

[Hook]
The seal is still whole
Nobody opens it
Nobody has to open it to know
The seal is still whole

[Verse 3]
And then that man's hand went into his coat
I saw that hand
I know what is in there
I read it for three days myself
Did that paper save these people?
It could not save them ten years ago
It cannot today either
That paper will not kill them
It will kill you

[Pre-Chorus]
Have we learned nothing?
There was a king of twelve years old
His uncle took the child away
Six said no and died for it
Six did not die — they walked out
They threw away their office and went to the mountains

[Chorus]
Dying is not the only refusal
Dying is not the only refusal
Dying is not the only refusal
We remember the six who died
We remember the six who walked out too
Nobody has forgotten them
Living is also a refusal
Walking out is also a refusal

[Verse 4]
He asked me
Then what do we do with these names?
Give them to me, I said
And what will you do, he asked
Nothing
Only I will not lose them
Until when, he asked
Until there is someone left to ask

[Break]
He took his hand out
It was empty
You did well

[Final Chorus]
Dying is not the only refusal
Dying is not the only refusal
Dying is not the only refusal
Iron remembers
And a person writes
And the paper waits
The seal is still whole
And still nobody has opened it

[Outro]
Snow is falling
So the ink does not freeze
I carry it inside my coat and walk
