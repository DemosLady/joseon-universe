Track 1: 돛을 내리다 (Lowering the Sail)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
Three days and we reach the capital
Load the rice and put out again
That was the kind of morning it was

[Verse 1]
Anchored in the harbour, the tide was wrong
Two sacks of rice, one barrel of water, that was all we needed
Inside my coat, a soaked ledger
Holding names the sea had kept for ten years
I am a sailor, so I did a sailor's work
Haggling over a price, counting the load
And then, at the far end of the dock, people gathered
The way they gathered was not good

[Hook]
I lower the sail, I lower the sail
Even with a wind, I lower it
I lower the sail, I lower the sail
Today the north is calling me

[Verse 2]
They said the north had been breached
They said the Jurchen had crossed the river
They said three fortresses had fallen in three days
They said refugees were already over the pass
And there was one more thing they said
They said the state was pardoning its criminals
That any man who could fight would not be asked about his past
That the men who spent their lives running were being called now

[Hook]
I lower the sail, I lower the sail
Even with a wind, I lower it
I lower the sail, I lower the sail
Today the north is calling me

[Verse 3]
The scholar asked, what do we do with this writing
I did not think long about the answer
This writing has waited ten years
What has waited ten years can wait one more day
But the people coming over that pass
Cannot wait for tonight
The scholar said nothing
The old fisherman said nothing either
That was the last time I saw the two of them

[Bridge]
I untied the halyard
The white sail came down slowly
The sail I had kept dyed black all my life
The sail I had raised white to be seen
Today I simply fold it
I leave the boat behind
The north is a place you walk to

[Final Hook]
I lower the sail, I lower the sail
Even with a wind, I lower it
I lower the sail, I lower the sail
Though the brush breaks, the ink does not dry
Ink knows how to wait
People cannot wait

[Outro]
From the dock I turned up the northern road
I lived my whole life on water
And my feet on the ground felt strange
I walked anyway

Track 3: 모루 (The Anvil)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
Iron does not cry
Beat it until it cries, and it cries

[Verse 1]
Three days now I have not let the fire out
The bellows breathes in a man's place
What they carted in from the villages is piled in the yard
I do not ask what that is
If it is iron, it will do

[Hook]
I melt it
I melt the bell
I melt it
I melt the chains
Iron does not ask what it used to be
And it does not ask what it will become
I do not make swords
I only melt down what is left

[Verse 2]
A bell came from the temple
A bell that had rung for a hundred years
The monk set it down and left without a word
Chains came from the magistrate's office
Who they had bound, I do not know
A pot came from somebody's house
A pot that had cooked rice
And iron came from the handle of some scholar's brush
It was small, so it became an arrowhead

[Break]
Iron remembers
Melt it and it still remembers
Even as a sword, there is a bell inside it

[Hook]
I melt it
I melt the bell
I melt it
I melt the chains
Iron does not ask what it used to be
And it does not ask what it will become
I do not make swords
I only melt down what is left

[Verse 3]
The young ones line up
Give me a sword
I told them it has not cooled yet
They said give it anyway
They took the hot thing in their bare hands and went
Their hands will have burned
On the road going north
There must be something more urgent than a hand

[Outro]
Fire burns
Salt preserves
Iron remembers
And I beat on the memory

Track 4: 빈 줄 (The Empty Line)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
They asked what a woman near fifty
Is going to a battlefield for
I said I am going to write

[Verse 1]
At sixteen I found paper under the floorboards
At thirty I taught children their letters
Past forty I became this village's teacher
And this winter
A man from the sea left a soaked ledger behind
He said they were the names of those who bought false testimony

[Pre-Chorus]
I read it
I read it for three days
And then I understood
These names are already too late

[Chorus]
I will not give these names to the world
The world is calling other names now
Instead, from today
I will write new names
The name of a man who is dying
On the day he dies
Before anyone can correct it

[Verse 2]
The road north was full of people
Men carrying iron, men carrying bows
Men walking with nothing at all
I walked carrying only paper and ink
People laughed. What will you do with that.
I will write, I said
Write what, they asked
I will write your name, I said
That man stopped laughing

[Pre-Chorus]
In ten years someone will ask
Who was there that day
And if nobody knows
It becomes a thing that never happened

[Chorus]
I will not give these names to the world
The world is calling other names now
Instead, from today
I will write new names
The name of a man who is dying
On the day he dies
Before anyone can correct it

[Bridge]
I thought about what to put on the first page
And in the end I just wrote the date
Beneath it I left one line empty
The empty line
Is the place for someone still alive

[Final Chorus]
Write the names
Write them while they are alive
Write them after death
And anyone can change them and no one will know
Fire burns
Salt preserves
Iron remembers
And a person writes

[Outro]
Snow is falling
So the ink does not freeze
I carry it inside my coat and walk
