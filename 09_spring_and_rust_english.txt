Track 1: 언 밭 (Frozen Field)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
They say we won
I asked what we get for it
Nobody answered

[Verse 1]
The ground is still frozen
The spade goes in and stops
Two years unploughed, so it has set hard
It takes three days to break ground like that
I did not go to fight
They said I was past the age
So I was here
I got through the winter here

[Hook]
They say spring has come
But the ground is still frozen
They say we won
But the pot is still empty
And still I plough
Because you plough or you don't eat
And still I plough
Because it is the only thing I know how to do

[Verse 2]
Last year we ate all the seed
There was nothing to eat in winter
So we ate what was meant for planting
Do you know what that means
It means there is nothing to plant this year
And still I plough the field
For when there is something to put in it
Waiting on what I don't have, digging what I do

[Hook]
They say spring has come
But the ground is still frozen
They say we won
But the pot is still empty
And still I plough
Because you plough or you don't eat
And still I plough
Because it is the only thing I know how to do

[Verse 3]
The spade caught on something
I thought it was a stone
It was not a stone

[Bridge]
It was a man
Lying face down
A piece of armour still hooked on one shoulder
And everything under it torn away
I took hold of that shoulder and turned him over

[Break]
It was my son

[Verse 4]
I sent him last autumn
They did not take him — he went
Twenty years I raised him in this field
And in three days he was gone
That day I could not say anything
I could not even say come back safe
And the words I did not say
sat in my throat all winter

[Final Hook]
They say spring has come
But the ground is still frozen
They say he is alive
That the breath is still in him
So I lifted him onto my back
He was light
Something I raised for twenty years
cannot be that light

[Outro]
I did not plough the field that day
I did not plough it the next day either
Three days I did not plough
And spring came anyway

Track 2: 소금 항아리 (The Salt Jar)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
Mother moves the salt jar
She moves it every morning
That sound is how I know it is morning

[Verse 1]
Forty days I lay there
Of the first fortnight I have no memory
They told me my father carried me home on his back
I have no memory of that either
The shoulder will not heal
On rainy days the bone cries
Mother boils cloth every day
And the steam fills the room

[Pre-Hook]
They say I came back alive
That is what the village says
And it is true that I came back alive
But I do not know what it is that came back

[Hook]
There is something inside my coat
I do not know what it is
A bundle of soaked paper
Full of letters, and I cannot read
If she had told me to throw it away I would have
She did not
She said keep it
That is all I have done

[Verse 2]
It was the day the fighting ended
A woman called me over from behind the tents
A woman who knew her letters
The one who wrote down the names of the dying
She had written on my arm too
I did not know then what it meant
She took something out of her coat
Wrapped in oiled cloth and wrapped again in hemp

[Quoted]
Hold this for me
I am forty-four and you are twenty
That is all it is
Do not ask what it is
If you know, it gets heavy

[Pre-Hook]
I asked why me
And she answered
Because it looks like you will live a long time
That sentence still frightens me

[Hook]
There is something inside my coat
I do not know what it is
A bundle of soaked paper
Full of letters, and I cannot read
If I could read I would have read it
And I don't know what I would have done then
Because I could not read, I kept it
I don't know if that is luck

[Verse 3]
Mother scoops salt in the kitchen
It is a big jar, up to her shoulder
All winter we lived on what was inside it
Salt does not spoil
And what is inside salt does not spoil either
On the day she went to market
I went into the kitchen
One hand still cannot take any weight
I went in anyway

[Bridge]
I took out half the salt
I laid the oiled cloth underneath
I covered it with the salt again
I closed the lid
I put the jar back where it stood
The position was slightly off
It took me a long while to set it straight

[Final Hook]
Fire burns
Salt preserves
I am not iron and I am not letters
I am just here
Mother moves the jar every morning
And listening to that sound I know
It is still there
It is still there

[Outro]
If someone asks one day
I can take it out then
If nobody ever asks
It can just stay there

[Intro]
My grandfather, see
he's eighty
and he's hard of hearing
so you have to ask him loudly

[Verse 1]
I go to lessons to learn my letters
and the teacher told us
to go and ask the old people about the old days
I've only got my grandfather so I went to him
Grandfather what was it like when you were young, I asked, loudly
And he starts telling me about the cow
how the cow was and how the field was
when it rained and when it didn't
No not that, I said, the war, the war
And when I said that he went quiet
And after a long time he says this

[Pre-Chorus]
Back then everyone went
Not because they told us to go
but because it felt like we couldn't not go
I was twenty

[Chorus]
The stories my grandfather told me
stories of a world I never saw
about a frozen field, about salt
about standing in a line
That's how it was then, he says
Everyone was like that then, he says
I didn't understand all of it
but I remember every bit of his voice

[Verse 2]
He showed me his shoulder, said it still hurts
said on rainy days the bone cries
That sounded funny so I laughed and he laughed too
But when he told me the next part his voice got smaller
He said my great-grandfather carried him home from the field
that he lay there forty days
that he doesn't even remember the first fortnight
Twenty is only five years older than me
and I can't imagine being like that at that age
I still find the water jar heavy

[Pre-Chorus]
And then a woman came into it
He said she was a woman who knew her letters
the one who wrote down the names of the dying
He said she left something with him

[Chorus]
The stories my grandfather told me
stories of a world I never saw
about a frozen field, about salt
about standing in a line
That's how it was then, he says
Everyone was like that then, he says
I didn't understand all of it
but I remember every bit of his voice

[Verse 3]
Left what with you, I said. Paper, he said.
What paper. Paper full of letters.
Did you read it. I can't read, he said.
Then who read it. Nobody.
Then where is it now

[Bridge]
Grandfather didn't say anything for a long time
I thought he hadn't heard
so I asked again, loudly

[Break]
I don't remember
he said

[Verse 4]
Whether he really didn't remember
or whether he remembered and said that anyway
I don't know
I should have asked one more time
But that day he said he was tired
so I just left
I told the teacher about the cow

[Final Chorus]
The stories my grandfather told me
stories of a world I never saw
I don't know where it is
I only know that it was
I don't know if that's enough
but here I am telling it again

[Outro]
My grandfather, see
he went to war at twenty
