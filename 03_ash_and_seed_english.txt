Track 1: 마지막 붓 (The Last Brush)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
This morning the dew is unusually clear
Even the grass at the execution ground knows it is spring

[Verse 1]
In the tribunal prison I counted a hundred days
Scratching the dates on the wall with my nails
At first I was angry. Then I was sad.
Now it is nothing at all. It is simply quiet.
No light enters a room without a window
But close my eyes and I see the sea at Jeju
That horizon I watched from exile
It had no end — like my writing

[Pre-Chorus]
They tell me today is the last
Good.
Because it is the last, there are things I can say

[Chorus]
Kill me
But my writing has already left
Cut off this head
But you cannot cut the mouths of the people
The brush ends here
But the ink has already soaked in
Not into paper
But into a thousand chests

[Verse 2]
My father put a brush in my hand
Winter, four years old, my hand so small
That the brush was bigger than it
My father said, one who writes
Lives longer than a king
I did not know then what those words weighed
Only now, in this cell, do I know
The king can kill me
But the king too will die one day
And if my writing lives a single day longer than the king
Then I have won

[Pre-Chorus]
If you ask whether I have regrets
I have none
There is only one thing I wanted

[Chorus]
Kill me
But my writing has already left
Cut off this head
But you cannot cut the mouths of the people
The brush ends here
But the ink has already soaked in
Not into paper
But into a thousand chests

[Bridge]
This is the one thing
I wanted to see one more spring
When the plum blossoms open on Jeju
That path where the petals blew in the wind
It was exile, but it was beautiful
I learned it here in prison
When I was free I did not know freedom
When I could hold a brush I did not know its weight
Now that both are gone, I can see both

[Chorus]
Kill me
This is my last piece of writing
Cut off this head
This is my last brush
But hear me, king
A shoot will break through the ash
On the paper you burned
New letters will bloom

[Outro]
I set down the brush
The ink dries
But the writing
The writing remains

Track 2: 길 위의 그림자 (Shadow on the Road)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
The market burned
When the soldiers came, only I got out
Why was it me

[Verse 1]
I turned my back on Hanyang and walked south
Threw away the mask. Threw away the drum.
Bare face, empty hands, standing on the road
If I am not a clown, what am I
In the first village I was given a cup of water
A woman asked me where I had come from
From the capital. What happened there.
I opened my mouth and the tears came first

[Pre-Chorus]
I have to tell about that night
Because someone has to remember
The scholar is dead
His writing stayed in my mouth

[Chorus]
I walk, from village to village
Walking with one shadow beside me
I speak, from person to person
I tell the story of the dead
The clown is dead
The storyteller was born
The laughter is gone
Truth sits in its place

[Verse 2]
In a tavern in Gyeongsang I met a farmer
"I know that gentleman. The one who wrote."
"That writing that came up from Jeju — I read it."
I nodded. He has passed away.
The farmer set down his cup
He was silent a long while
And then he said, I remember that writing
I read it to my son as well
In that moment I understood
The scholar's ink had not dried
It was still flowing — through my mouth

[Pre-Chorus]
Passing Songni Mountain I saw the hermitage
A nun came down and handed me a cup of tea
Snow fell on her shaved head
We smiled without speaking. We knew each other.

[Chorus]
I walk, from village to village
Walking with one shadow beside me
I speak, from person to person
I tell the story of the dead
The clown is dead
The storyteller was born
The laughter is gone
Truth sits in its place

[Bridge]
Sometimes at night, alone by a fire
I hear the scholar's voice
"What you cannot kill is a thought"
And I hear the shouting of the market
"The market street, the market street"
That sound still rings in my ears
I survived
Whether that is a blessing or a punishment I still don't know
I only walk
Because the story is not finished

[Chorus]
I walk, from village to village
Even when this road ends, the story remains
I speak, from person to person
Planting them one at a time, like seeds
The clown is dead
The storyteller will die one day too
But the story
The story never stops walking

[Outro]
One step
And another step
The road does not end

Track 3: 빈 산 (The Empty Mountain)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
The mountain took me back
Going down and coming up
The same road, but different feet

[Verse 1]
The night I went down carrying a torch
Has drifted away like a dream
The voice I raised before the palace
Came back to the mountain as an echo
The smell of smoke was in my robe
Three washings and it was still there
On the fourth I let it go
Not the smell — it was me who let go

[Pre-Chorus]
I took up the wooden bell again
The same bell, a different sound
Before, I struck it to cover the anger
Now I simply strike it

[Chorus]
The empty mountain holds me
An empty heart holds the mountain
The anger has left
I didn't let it go — it burned itself out
I went down carrying a torch
And came back with empty hands
That empty hands could be this light
I never knew, my whole life

[Verse 2]
Spring came
The plum trees blossomed
This time they are beautiful
My mother's garden comes to mind
But it doesn't hurt. It simply comes.
In the summer rain I hear my father's voice
I hear it. And I smile.
Autumn leaves fall
Not fire — just leaves
Let winter come
And let winter go

[Pre-Chorus]
Yesterday a man came up from below
A man with a stick, roads carved into his face
I handed him a cup of tea
We didn't speak, but we knew — we came out of the same fire

[Chorus]
The empty mountain holds me
An empty heart holds the mountain
The anger has left
I didn't let it go — it burned itself out
The prayer came back
The same words, a different meaning
Namu Amitabul
This time it is a real prayer

[Bridge]
I once asked the Buddha
What if the anger will not let go of me
Now I know the answer
It was not about letting the anger go
It was about burning it all the way through
On the mountain, in prayer, it could not all burn
I had to go down and become a torch
When it had all burned, only ash was left
Snow falls on the ash
And when the snow melts, grass grows there

[Chorus]
The empty mountain holds me
An empty heart holds the mountain
Namu Amitabul
This time I can say it without tears
Namu Amitabul
This time I don't have to clench my teeth
I let go
And the mountain answered

[Outro]
I said that even if the mountain did not answer, I would keep asking
Today the mountain answered
With wind

Track 4: 첫 번째 새싹 (The First Sprout)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
Grandmother told me
That long ago one piece of writing almost changed the world
I asked her, where is that writing
Grandmother smiled
Go and find it

[Verse 1]
I grew up between rice fields in a small village in Jeolla
I never learned to read. Because I'm a girl.
My father put a plough in my hands, not a brush
But ears cannot be stopped
When I was small a strange old man came to the market
Walking village to village with a stick
Telling stories
About palaces and scholars and fire and ink
The children thought it was a frightening old tale
Only I knew it was real

[Pre-Chorus]
The last time that man came
He took my hand and said
"This story — you will carry it on"
I didn't know then what he meant

[Chorus]
Beneath the ash there was a seed
No one knew. I didn't know either.
Then rain came, and spring came
And it broke through the soil
I am the first sprout
You thought it burned, you thought it ended
But here it is
A new letter risen from the earth

[Verse 2]
Spring of my sixteenth year, cleaning grandmother's room
Under the floorboards I found something wrapped in cloth
I opened it — hanji. There were letters on it.
I couldn't read them, but I could feel them
The smell of ink was still there
Decades had passed and it was still there
I took it to the village teacher
He read for a long time and then wept
Is this that scholar's writing
Is this the writing that tried to wake the world

[Pre-Chorus]
That night I said to the teacher
Please teach me to write
He said a girl may not
Every dawn I sat at his door
On the morning of the third day, the door opened

[Chorus]
Beneath the ash there was a seed
No one knew. I didn't know either.
Then rain came, and spring came
And it broke through the soil
I am the first sprout
You thought it burned, you thought it ended
But here it is
A new letter risen from the earth

[Bridge]
The day I first held a brush
My hand shook
I ground the ink and drew a first stroke on the hanji
It came out crooked
And still, it was a letter
The scholar died, but the brush is here
The clown vanished, but the story is here
The nun went back to the mountain, but the ember is here
Under the floorboards
And in my hand

[Chorus]
Beneath the ash there was a seed
That seed was me
Rain came, and spring came
And at last I came out
I am the first sprout
I don't know how far this writing will go
But I write
I carry on what the scholar began

[Outro]
Ink and embers
Ash and seed
This is not the end
It is the beginning
