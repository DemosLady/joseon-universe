Track 1: 왕의 길목 (The King's Path)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
I'm walking the path the king once walked
Stones beneath my feet, ghosts in the fog
The dust of dynasties on my tongue

[Verse 1]
Ink drips from the brush, I write my own royal decree
His Majesty never kneels, and neither do I
Hanyang night, moonlight spilling between the roof tiles
Scholars hiding under the censors' watchful eyes
I unfold my verses like a state exam paper
I set my own law above the Three Bonds and Five Virtues
The janggu is my heartbeat, never once off rhythm
Palace or back alley, it's the same game

[Pre-Chorus]
Eolssigu — I feel it deep in my bones
From Gyeongbokgung to the alleys of Jongno, I was never alone

[Chorus]
I'm walking the king's path, fog upon the mountains
Every step a dynasty, every breath a spring
A crown made of moonlight, a sword made of sound
Joseon runs in my veins, I stand guarding this land

[Verse 2]
Hanbok turned inside out, armor beneath the silk
Tongue like a sword, flow like silk
They tried to shut me outside the four great gates
I carved my name upon them — now that's history
The taepyeongso screams in place of my mouth
Five hundred years of fire condensed into this one day
Like a petition to the throne, not one letter wasted
The red seal — it's a royal command, stand back

[Bridge]
Kindling embers along the mountain ridges
Summon the shaman, cross the bridge
Between the living and the dead
Between dusk and dawn
Between silence and song
Carrying the will to benefit all mankind

[Chorus]
I'm walking the king's path, fog upon the mountains
Every step a dynasty, every breath a spring
A crown made of moonlight, a sword made of sound
Joseon runs in my veins, I stand guarding this land

[Outro]
Stones beneath my feet
Ghosts in the fog
The path remembers
Even when we forget

Track 2: 빈 궁궐 메아리 (Echoes of the Empty Palace)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
The wind blows in
The voice that once filled the empty palace
Now only an echo remains

[Verse 1]
Deep night in Gyeongbokgung, sitting before a single candle
Even on the throne, the seat beside me is empty
Only now do I understand King Danjong's sorrow
It's not the crown that's heavy, it's my heart
A heart even the Royal Secretariat's diary cannot record
Call the royal physician — this sickness has no cure
Only the court ladies' footsteps echo down the halls
And in those footsteps I keep searching for yours

[Pre-Chorus]
Your Majesty, let me go
The moonlight is growing cold
Your Majesty, let me go
The throne is freezing over

[Chorus]
Sitting alone on the frozen throne
Father of ten thousand people, child of one woman
Snow falls and covers all, but your seat never melts
I could rule a thousand years and still never hold your hand

[Verse 2]
You left like Queen Hyeondeok, without a word
Even Sejong lost his daughter-in-law and hid inside letters
I don't even have letters, only sound remains
Swallowing tears over the banquet at Taepyeonggwan
Even poison by royal decree cannot kill this pain
The han of deposed Queen Yun lives again inside me
The royal shrine music plays, but I hear only your voice
I burn incense in the funerary hall, calling you every night

[Bridge]
I should never have become king
I should have been born a nobleman's son
I should have walked beneath Gwangtong Bridge holding your hand
I should have called your name in the season of lotus blooms
Flowers bloom even on the road to the afterlife
Your Majesty, do not weep

[Chorus]
Sitting alone on the frozen throne
Father of ten thousand people, child of one woman
Snow falls and covers all, but your seat never melts
I could rule a thousand years and still never hold your hand

[Outro]
The wind blows in
Your Majesty
Let me go

Track 3: 도깨비불 (Ghost Fire)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
The wind blows over the palace walls
He cannot sleep again tonight

[Verse 1]
Through the paper window I see a shadow
That man on the throne, staring only at the candle
I reach out but cannot touch — the wall of the living world
Letters from the afterlife arrive only as wind
The court ladies dare not speak my name
Not a deposed queen, not quite a ghost
My face reflects on the pond of Gyeonghoeru
Every time he looks at the water, he brushes past me

[Pre-Chorus]
Your Majesty, I am here
Even unseen, I am here

[Chorus]
I drift like a dokkaebi fire
Moonlight hanging from the palace eaves
Only into His Majesty's dreams can I enter
When he wakes, this body vanishes again

[Verse 2]
No memorial tablet for me at Jongmyo — a woman erased from the records
A love that vanished without one line in the Annals
And yet every night he burns incense before the funerary hall
Incense I cannot receive — but his heart still reaches me
The ghost of Seonjuk Bridge remains a symbol of loyalty
But I couldn't leave a name behind, not even for love
Bound by han, I cannot leave
I count the nights beside the sound of his breathing

[Bridge]
I didn't leave first
I was taken
Your Majesty, it was not my fault

[Chorus]
I drift like a dokkaebi fire
Moonlight hanging from the palace eaves
Only into His Majesty's dreams can I enter
When he wakes, this body vanishes again

[Outro]
The wind blows
Your Majesty
I am here

Track 4: 피의 옥새 (The Blood Seal)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
Bring me the historians' drafts
Give me the names

[Verse 1]
The gentle king is dead — from today, a tyrant
Before the blood dries on Jaseondang's floor, call the next name
Shall I show you what the Gapja Purge means
Those who killed my woman — I'll end three generations of their line
Censors' heads roll and still you file petitions?
On those petitions I'll stamp blood instead of my seal
The Chief State Councillor on his knees is not enough
Drag in your father, and your son too

[Chorus]
I stamp the blood seal
No forgiveness, no mercy
I stamp the blood seal
Even if Joseon drowns in blood, I will not stop

[Verse 2]
They say Yeonsangun was mad? There was a reason
The day my mother, deposed Queen Yun, drank the poison
That poison flowed into her son
Blood remembers — royal blood never forgets
Did Grand Prince Suyang weep when he killed his nephew?
Wipe the tears, sit on the throne — that is what a king is
Sharpen the blade while smiling, like Queen Insu
Inside the palace, family is the deadliest thing

[Bridge]
Royal Secretariat, write this down
From today, the records
Will be written by the king himself
Rewrite the Annals
Write the history I demand
(laugh — silence)

[Verse 3]
Open the coffin — look at the face of the love you killed
Remember that face as you go to hell
Nosan-gun's han? Nothing compared to mine
I stand upon five hundred years of Joseon's history of blood
I walked the king's path and it led me here
The echoes of the empty palace have turned to screams
The ghost fire becomes a torch and burns the palace down
The blood seal — this is my final decree

[Outro]
...It's quiet
Quiet, at last
