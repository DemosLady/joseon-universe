Track 1: 북두칠성 (Big Dipper)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
The Big Dipper does not change its place
Every other star turns
But those seven stay in the north
That is why a sailor trusts only them

[Verse 1]
All my life I rowed water roads in secret
Today, for the first time, I row north
I learned these routes in order to hide
Now I use them in order to show
I raise the sail — not a black one
A white sail that hides nothing
Let anyone see. Today
It is not the wind's fault. It is mine.

[Hook]
Big Dipper, Big Dipper
Seven that do not change their place
Big Dipper, Big Dipper
Today we do not turn back either

[Verse 2]
Forty years I knew this sea
And today, for the first time, it is strange to me
Now I know where my son was
The waves did not take him
People sank that ship
Ten years I lived blaming the sea
Forgive me, sea. It was not you.
Now I go to find the ones I should have blamed
This old body, I don't know how far it will get
But I will call my son's name once before I die

[Hook]
Big Dipper, Big Dipper
Seven that do not change their place
Big Dipper, Big Dipper
Today we do not turn back either

[Verse 3]
Inside my coat there are two copies
The salt-eaten original and the one I wrote out
One is evidence and one is a life
So that if either is lost, the other remains
Ten years ago a scholar walked this road
He took up his brush and went to the execution ground
I have what he never had
The names of those who killed him are here

[Bridge]
When we reach land, what becomes of me
I have nothing left to lose
I only have to deliver this writing
Then it's enough — row

[Final Hook]
Big Dipper, Big Dipper
Seven that do not change their place
Big Dipper, Big Dipper
Today we do not turn back either
The sea kept it for ten years
Can people not keep it for one day

[Outro]
And then I looked behind me
And that woman was standing at the bow
Looking toward the island

Track 2: 물로 돌아간 여자 (The Woman Who Returned to the Water)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
The island was going down below the horizon
That was when she stood up

[Verse 1]
No one had asked her to come
She climbed aboard on her own
They were the hands that brought the documents up
So she must have wanted to see it through
But after half a day of rowing
She stood at the bow
Looking not forward but back
Watching the island go away

[Hook]
The woman who returned to the water
We did not hold her back
The woman who returned to the water
Because that sea was her dry land

[Verse 2]
The scholar asked, what is it
She answered, smiling
When I get to the mainland, what am I
A woman with no letters and no name
Here I am the master
There I would have to lower my head
Your fight is a fight of the land
I have already finished mine
I met the bones and I brought up the names
My part ends here

[Hook]
The woman who returned to the water
We did not hold her back
The woman who returned to the water
Because that sea was her dry land

[Verse 3]
She set down the float
She left the iron pick behind
Like someone with nothing to take
She retied the ties of her jacket
And looked at the three of us, once each
To the old fisherman she bowed her head
Because she knew his son's name
To the scholar she said nothing
Because everything to say was already written
And then she went into the water
It made no sound

[Bridge]
A long while later
Far off, one breath-whistle was heard
It meant: alive
We took up the oars again

[Final Hook]
The woman who returned to the water
We did not hold her back
The woman who returned to the water
Because that sea was her dry land
We went north
And she went down
Both were the way home

[Outro]
The island disappeared completely
But she had not disappeared
She had gone back

Track 3: 산문 앞에서 (At the Temple Gate)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
Three men stood before the temple gate
They were men who smelled of the sea

[Verse 1]
We reached land and walked three days into the mountains
We were told to find a small hermitage
The woman who stood before the palace with a torch
Now strikes a wooden bell on a mountain, they said
I took the soaked ledger from my coat
The names the salt had half eaten
She turned one page, then another
And her hand stopped

[Pre-Chorus]
Do you know this name
She raised her head
Know it? Of course
That is the hand that took my father

[Chorus]
The fire has all burned. The ash has gone cold.
But the memory did not burn
The fire has all burned. My hands are empty.
But the names did not burn
What I have to give is not fire
It is what I saw

[Verse 2]
She went down the names with her finger
This one stood beside the queen dowager
This one came to my father's funeral
This one held my hand and wept
They are all faces I saw in the palace as a child
Who wrote it, I do not know
But who ordered it — that I know
The ledger has only names
Inside me there are faces

[Pre-Chorus]
Will you come with us, we asked
She was silent a long while
We were asking a woman who had lived ten years on a mountain
To come down again

[Chorus]
The fire has all burned. The ash has gone cold.
But the memory did not burn
The fire has all burned. My hands are empty.
But the names did not burn
What I have to give is not fire
It is what I saw

[Bridge]
When I was young I carried a torch
With that fire I saved nothing
I burned it all and came back with empty hands
That was when I learned — anger does not last
But memory lasts
I am no longer fire
I am a witness

[Final Chorus]
The fire has all burned. The ash has gone cold.
But the memory did not burn
Though the brush breaks, the ink does not dry
And the names did not burn
What I have to give is not fire
It is what I saw

[Outro]
We came down the mountain at dawn
Looking back
The hermitage door stood open
There was no one inside

Track 4: 이어도 (Ieodo)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
They say the sea knows everything
They say it simply does not tell
But today
I will tell

[Verse 1]
I stayed on the island. I did not go to the mainland.
They went north and I went down
I take the float in my arms and enter the water again
Today I am not going for abalone
Today I am going to call

[Refrain]
Ieodo sana
Ieodo sana

Ieodo sana
Those who left their names behind
Ieodo sana

[Verse 2]
There were twelve people in that split hull
Somebody's son, somebody's husband
The government's documents had no names
But their mothers knew every one
The old fisherman's elder son — that name
I brought it up with me from under the water
His father went north
He went to call his son's name

[Refrain]
Ieodo sana
Ieodo sana
The island you go to and do not return from
Ieodo sana
Those who left their names behind
Ieodo sana

[Bridge]
The man who set down his brush and went to the execution ground
The man who threw away his mask and walked the roads
The woman who took up a torch and came down from the mountain
The child who found the writing beneath the floorboards
I never saw them
But their story came as far as this island
The sea is wide, but words still cross it
As there was a seed beneath the ash
There were names beneath the water

[Final Refrain]
Ieodo sana, ieodo sana
The island you go to and do not return from
Ieodo sana, ieodo sana
Those who left their names behind
Though the brush breaks, the ink does not dry

[Outro]
On clear nights
The northern horizon is red
Beacon or sunset, I do not know
Whether they arrived, I do not know
The sea does not tell
I will wait here
