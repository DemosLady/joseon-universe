Track 1: 붓끝에 칼 (A Blade at the Brush Tip)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
One stroke holds the truth
What the king feared was not the sword — it was the ink

[Verse 1]
The hand that held a brush in the Confucian academy yard
Top of the state examination, my name went up on the board
I stood in the Office of Censors and spoke plainly
One petition, and my rank was gone
The name they called loyal, overnight
Was swapped for a traitor's tag
At the tribunal gate I did not bow my head
If writing the truth is a crime, I will take it gladly

[Pre-Chorus]
A thousand ri of exile road on foot
Thrown into the wind of Jeju
The brush broke, but the fingers remained
Written in dirt, writing is still writing

[Chorus]
A blade hidden at the brush tip
One word wakes a thousand people
Before the ink dries
This truth climbs the palace wall
Erase it, burn it
From the ashes the letters bloom again
King, be afraid
What you cannot kill is a thought

[Verse 2]
One inkstone hidden in a one-room thatched house
Night after night I drew the world again on hanji
By day, playing fisherman, casting the net
By night, a scholar's hand, taking up the brush again
A student came and knelt before me
"Master, your writing has spread to the capital"
Mouth to mouth, market to tavern
The words the king erased became the people's song

[Pre-Chorus]
No paper — I write on the wall
No wall — I send it on the wind
Power is afraid of ink
And ink does not kneel before power

[Chorus]
A blade hidden at the brush tip
One word wakes a thousand people
Before the ink dries
This truth climbs the palace wall
Erase it, burn it
From the ashes the letters bloom again
King, be afraid
What you cannot kill is a thought

[Bridge]
Even when the king orders the histories rewritten
You can break a brush, but you cannot break the truth
The road Jo Gwang-jo walked
The nights Jeong Yak-yong endured
In this place of exile I follow after them
History is not written by the king
It is written by the letters that survive

[Chorus]
A blade hidden at the brush tip
One word wakes a thousand people
Before the ink dries
This truth climbs the palace wall
Erase it, burn it
From the ashes the letters bloom again
King, be afraid
What you cannot kill is a thought

[Outro]
The wind carries the letters
From a place of exile a thousand ri away
My writing blooms on the walls of Hanyang
The brush breaks, but the ink does not dry

Track 3: 산사의 여인 (The Woman at the Mountain Temple)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
Namu Amitabul
Namu Amitabul
Even if the mountain does not answer, I keep asking

[Verse 1]
In a hermitage deep in a valley of Songni Mountain
Moonlight falls on a shaved head
The day I took off the silk jeogori and put on the robe
The face in the mirror did not know me
Born in the palace, raised in the palace
Learning etiquette beside the queen dowager
The night the purge broke, my father was dragged away
My mother became a slave, and I became a dead woman

[Pre-Chorus]
If I read the sutras, can I forget
If I strike the wooden bell, will the anger go
A thousand recitations, and still, eyes closed
My father's scream drowns out the scripture

[Chorus]
Namu Amitabul, Namu Amitabul
Buddha, this is not a prayer
Namu Amitabul, Namu Amitabul
This is a scream through clenched teeth
Burn scars beneath the robe
This body scorched by the palace fire
You told me to forgive
But I cannot return to a burned house

[Verse 2]
Spring comes and the plum blossom is not beautiful
When the flowers open I see my mother's garden
In the summer rain I hear my father's voice
In autumn leaves I see our house burning
Only winter gives me peace
Because a frozen world is like my heart
From the village below, a rumor climbs
That inside a song the market clown sings
The truth of the palace is hidden
The truth I know. The truth I lived.

[Pre-Chorus]
If I read the sutras, can I forget
If I strike the wooden bell, will the anger go
A thousand recitations, and still, eyes closed
The smell of blood beats the smell of incense

[Chorus]
Namu Amitabul, Namu Amitabul
Buddha, this is not a prayer
Namu Amitabul, Namu Amitabul
This is a scream through clenched teeth
Burn scars beneath the robe
This body scorched by the palace fire
You told me to forgive
But I cannot return to a burned house

[Bridge]
At twenty-three I shaved my head
The world killed me, so I killed the world
But that is a lie
Hide in the mountain and the anger follows
Try to cover it with the wooden bell
And the heart beats louder than the bell
I ask you, Buddha
You told me to let go of the anger
But what if the anger will not let go of me

[Chorus]
Namu Amitabul, Namu Amitabul
Buddha, this is not a prayer
Namu Amitabul, Namu Amitabul
This is the roar of one who survived
Tear off the robe and this body still remembers
Flames and screams and the beam that fell
I cannot forgive
But I will go on living here

[Outro]
Namu Amitabul
Namu Amitabul

Track 4: 마지막 봉화 (The Last Signal Fire)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro]
The beacons are lit
The first fire on Jeju
The second on Songni Mountain
The third in the market street
The fourth right here, in your chest

[Verse 1]
In exile I wrote my last piece
This time not a poem but a call to rise
Ten years I held it in, ten years I hid it
The blade hidden at the brush tip, I draw it now
The Jeju wind carries this writing out
It reached a clown's hands and rose to the people's mouths
There is no more time to write in ink
The night has come when letters become embers

[Pre-Chorus]
I came down from the mountain
I did not take off the robe — this is my armor
Instead of the wooden bell I took up a torch
The praying is over. Now it is my turn.
You told me to let go of the anger
But tonight the anger lights the road

[Chorus]
Raise the signal fire, raise the signal fire
From mountain to mountain the fire spreads
Raise the signal fire, raise the signal fire
One person's ember becomes ten thousand fires
The scholar's ink became the clown's song
The clown's song became the people's roar
We hide no longer
Tonight, we are the beacon

[Verse 2]
I took off the mask, I am a clown no more
Snapped the drumstick and traded it for a torch
Ten years smiling with poison in my mouth
These feet that mimicked a nobleman's walk
Walk toward the palace today
The people of the market street follow behind
The guards draw swords and still we do not stop
A thousand voices weigh more than one blade

[Pre-Chorus]
Beyond the palace wall the shouting carries
King, open your window
The writing you erased has come back
The people you killed have come back
Like letters blooming out of ash
We are the thought you could not kill

[Chorus]
Raise the signal fire, raise the signal fire
From mountain to mountain the fire spreads
Raise the signal fire, raise the signal fire
One person's ember becomes ten thousand fires
The scholar's ink became the clown's song
The clown's song became the people's roar
We hide no longer
Tonight, we are the beacon

[Bridge]
I began with a brush
I began with a prayer
The writing became a song
The prayer became fire
The ink does not dry
The anger does not go out
We began apart
But tonight we are one fire

[Chorus]
Raise the signal fire, raise the signal fire
Across all eight provinces the fires are up
Raise the signal fire, raise the signal fire
Not even rain puts this one out
The ink of the exile became the prayer of the temple
The prayer of the temple woke the market street
And the market street woke the whole country
Tonight Joseon opens its eyes

[Outro]
The beacon goes out
But the ember remains
Ink and embers
Ink and embers
