The King's Path

The dust of dynasties on my tongue

오백 년 불이 오늘 하루에 응축돼
상소문처럼 한 글자도 허투루 안 써
붉은 도장 — 어명이다, 물러서라

Hanbok turned inside out, armor beneath the silk
Tongue like a sword, flow like silk
They tried to shut me outside the four great gates
I carved my name upon them — now that's history
The taepyeongso screams in place of my mouth
Five hundred years of fire condensed into this one day
Like a petition to the throne, not one letter wasted
The red seal — it's a royal command, stand back

산줄기 위에 불씨를 피워
무당을 불러, 다리를 건너
산 자와 죽은 자 사이
해질녘과 새벽 사이
침묵과 노래 사이

Kindling embers along the mountain ridges
Summon the shaman, cross the bridge
Between the living and the dead
Between dusk and dawn
Between silence and song
Carrying the will to benefit all mankind

왕의 길을 걷고 있어, 산 위에 안개
